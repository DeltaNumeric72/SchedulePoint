HONESTY NOTE ON P2's 288 PROBES
Three of the twelve tables report `0 visible` inside an owned tenant:
capability_grants, audit_checkpoints, outbox_effects. Those three probes are
therefore VACUOUS in this prototype — a probe over a table it cannot see
reports "0 wrong" for the boring reason, which is precisely the failure mode
`red-cases/probe-is-not-vacuous.test.ts` exists to catch. The cause is the
prototype's probe context, not the mechanism:

  capability_grants   its policies are group-scoped or own-scoped; an
                      organization-scoped context with no acting membership
                      reads none (asserted directly by authz/schema.test.ts).
  audit_checkpoints   written by app_worker only; this prototype provisions
                      under app_runtime and writes no checkpoint.
  outbox_effects      likewise app_worker's alone.

So P2 establishes zero cross-tenant leakage on NINE tables with rows actually
visible, and establishes nothing on the other three. Closing that gap is not
this prototype's job — it is deliverable B (the SBX-004 sweep), which probes
under every role and every context class. Recorded here so the number 288 is
not read as 288 meaningful probes.
